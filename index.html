How it works · Bluetooth laser

Point. Click. It's in the window.

01

Pair

Connect a supported Bluetooth laser measure to WinCraft once.

02

Point & click

Aim the laser at the opening and press the button on the meter.

03

It fills itself

Width and height land in the window. Nothing to read out, nothing to type again.

No laser? Type the width and height and watch the drawing update. Bluetooth support is limited to supported models.

What you get

From the site to the quotation.

A project for every site

Client, address, map link, date and site notes. Add every opening by floor and room, each with its own mark.

Design any window

Sliding from 2 to 8 shutters, casement, tilt and turn, top and bottom hung, fixed panels and louvres, with your own divisions.

Full specifications

Series and profiles, glass, lock and bearing brands, and a powder coating shade library with colour preview.

Quotation PDF

Your logo, window drawings, specifications, prices and totals. GST shown inclusive or exclusive.

Worksheet & Excel

A measurement book PDF with a checklist, plus Excel schedules. Save or share straight from the app.

Your rates, your totals

You enter quantity and unit rate; WinCraft totals the project. Works in millimetres, inches or feet.
