Questions

Which devices does the beta run on?

Android phones and tablets. The beta is Android only for now.

Do I need a laser measure?

No. Type the width and height and the drawing updates. A supported Bluetooth laser just fills them for you.

Does WinCraft set my prices?

No. You enter quantity and unit rate, and WinCraft totals the project.

Where are my projects kept?

On your device. No sign-in, no ads, with a daily backup and backup to a file you keep.
