WinCraft Privacy Policy

Who we are

WinCraft is an Android app for preparing window measurements, worksheets and quotations. It is developed by Anand Patadiya. For privacy questions or requests, email wincraftsupport@gmail.com.

Projects and company details

WinCraft stores the information you enter on your device. This can include client names and contact details, site addresses, measurements, photos, specifications and prices, and the company branding and contact details you use in documents. WinCraft does not require an account. We do not operate a server that receives your projects, and the app does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Site location and maps

If you request your current location, WinCraft uses the location available through Android to fill in a site map link. Coordinates can remain in the saved link until you replace or remove it or delete the site. You can also pin a location manually. Opening the in-app map requests map resources from OpenFreeMap. These requests expose your IP address and can indicate the area being viewed. OpenFreeMap says its error logs may contain IP addresses and requested URLs for seven days. It can temporarily log IP addresses during security investigations for up to 30 days. If you search for an address or place, WinCraft passes the text to Android’s geocoder. The provider and its handling of the query can vary by device. When you open a saved map link, the selected maps service receives the information in the link. You can manage location permission in Android Settings.

Bluetooth laser measures

WinCraft uses Bluetooth to connect to a supported laser measure and apply readings to window dimensions. Android 12 and later use Nearby Devices permission for scanning and connecting. Android 11 and earlier may require location permission to discover Bluetooth devices. WinCraft uses that permission for discovery when connecting to a meter. Saved measurements are stored with your project.

Backups, transfers and sharing

If Android backup is enabled, Android can back up selected project data, settings and quotation revisions to your backup account. WinCraft keeps up to seven automatic daily recovery copies on the device and excludes them from Android cloud backup. Android device-to-device transfer may include daily recovery copies on Android 12 and later. If you export a backup or document, the destination you choose receives the file. If you share a file or text, the receiving app or recipient can retain a copy.

Retention, deletion and security

WinCraft keeps saved data in Android’s app storage until you remove it or clear the app’s data. Deleted windows may remain temporarily in the in-app recovery list. Backups, exported files and shared copies can persist separately. Manage those copies through Android backup settings or the app, account or storage location holding them. Protect your device and backup account with the security settings available to you. Contact us at wincraftsupport@gmail.com for help with privacy questions or requests.

Photos, logos and imported backups

WinCraft processes photos you take or select, logos you choose for company branding, and backup files you choose to restore. Photos and logos can appear in project records or generated documents. A backup you restore can bring back information deleted since the backup was made.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact us, we receive your email address, message and anything you attach. We use this information to respond, resolve the issue and handle privacy requests. We keep support correspondence and any remaining attachments for up to 12 months after the request is closed. We delete attachments sooner when they are no longer needed. We may retain relevant records longer where required by law or necessary to resolve an active dispute. Please remove client details from screenshots and files before sending them unless they are needed to resolve your issue.

Your choices and rights

You can review, correct or delete project information in WinCraft and manage permissions in Android Settings. Depending on where you live and what information we handle, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, receive a copy of, restrict or object to processing of your information, or to withdraw consent where processing relies on it. You can contact us at wincraftsupport@gmail.com or complain to your local privacy regulator where applicable. If a business entered your details into its project, contact that business about its project records.

International use and children

WinCraft’s developer is based in India. Depending on the services you use, map, search, backup and support information may be processed in other countries. WinCraft is intended for professional use and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

We’ll update this policy when WinCraft’s data handling changes.
